The structural shift

Verifiable pathways, not speculation.

Our data tools have helped formalize over a thousand athletes. Imara is managing 50+ clubs across Kenya and Rwanda, reducing administrative noise by 70%. We are building verifiable pathways that directly contribute to the African sports economy.

True impact happens when you build infrastructure that empowers the school league just as much as the professional club.

How we measure it

Three layers of change.

Athlete mobility

Digitized career portfolios that remove scouting barriers and give emerging talent the metrics and visibility they need to scale up.

Institutional capacity

Clubs and federations running on professional tools — attendance, payments, scheduling and welfare tracking in one place.

Economic formalization

A connected marketplace that turns scattered sporting activity into a measurable, investable economy.
